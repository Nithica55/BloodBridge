🚨 The Problem

During emergency situations, patients and their families may struggle to identify potential blood donors quickly. BloodBridge aims to make donor discovery faster by organizing donor information according to blood group and location.

How BloodBridge Works

1️⃣ Register

Potential donors can register their blood group, location and availability.

2️⃣ Request

A patient or authorized requester can submit an emergency blood requirement.

3️⃣ Match

BloodBridge identifies potential donors using blood group and location-based matching.

4️⃣ Connect

Potential matches can be contacted for further verification and coordination.
